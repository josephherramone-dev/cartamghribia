Match

Play a card with the same suit or number as the top card. Can't? Draw one and your turn ends.

2 · Hezz jouj

The next player draws 2, unless they answer with another 2. Twos stack: 2, 4, 6…

1 · Skip

The next player loses their turn.

7 · Wild

Play it on anything and choose the next suit.
