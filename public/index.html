Rules
  • Each player gets 4 cards. Match the top card's suit or number.
  • Can't play? Draw one card and your turn ends.
  • 2 — next player draws 2, unless they answer with another 2 (they stack!).
  • 1 — skips the next player.
  • 7 — wild: play it on anything and choose the next suit.
  • First to empty their hand wins.
